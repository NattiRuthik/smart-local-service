# LOCALHELP AI — FINAL MEGA PROMPT

Build and modify the existing **LocalHelp AI** website into a highly interactive, modern, mobile-first, AI-powered local services and roadside assistance platform for India.

The website must feel like a **real intelligent service assistant**, not a static service directory.

The core experience is:

**Customer has a problem**
↓
**Customer describes/scans/speaks about the problem**
↓
**AI understands the problem**
↓
**AI asks useful follow-up questions**
↓
**AI identifies the likely service**
↓
**AI provides an OPTIONAL "Before You Call" checklist**
↓
**Customer checks only what they know or can safely check**
↓
**AI reassesses using the information provided**
↓
**AI recommends the appropriate service**
↓
**Customer sees nearby providers**
↓
**Customer selects a provider**
↓
**Customer explicitly shares location**
↓
**Provider receives the service request + customer location**
↓
**Provider accepts**
↓
**Customer and provider can see service progress/live location**
↓
**Provider reaches customer**
↓
**Service is completed**
↓
**Location sharing automatically stops**
↓
**Customer can rate/review the provider**

The two signature features of LocalHelp AI must be:

### 1. AI BEFORE THE SERVICE

AI helps the customer understand the problem and safely prepare before contacting a professional.

### 2. LIVE LOCATION DURING THE SERVICE

After the customer explicitly approves location sharing, the provider can receive the customer's location and the customer can track the provider while they are on the way.

# 4. AI PROBLEM ANALYSIS

When the customer enters a problem, do NOT immediately send them to a provider.

First show an AI analysis panel.

Example:

Customer:

> "My bike stopped suddenly and won't start."

Display:

### AI Understanding

**Possible issue:** Vehicle breakdown

**Likely service:** Bike Mechanic

**Other possibilities:** Battery / Fuel / Electrical issue

**Confidence:** Moderate

Then:

**"Before contacting a mechanic, let's check a few safe things first."**

Never present a diagnosis as certain.

Use:

* "Possible issue"
* "Likely service"
* "This may indicate"
* "One possibility is"
* "Based on the information provided"
* "I can't confirm the exact fault remotely"

# 6. CRITICAL CHECKLIST BEHAVIOR

Implement this exactly.

### Each checklist item is independent.

The customer can:

* Check one item
* Check several items
* Check all items
* Leave some items unanswered
* Leave all items unanswered
* Choose "I don't know"
* Skip the entire checklist

The customer must NEVER be blocked because checklist items are unanswered.

The customer must always be able to continue using:

**Continue**

**Get AI Recommendation**

**Find a Service**

or

**Skip Checklist**

even if:

**0 checklist items are completed.**

---

# 7. VERY IMPORTANT — UNCHECKED DOES NOT MEAN NO

This logic is mandatory.

### Unchecked

Means:

**"Not answered."**

It does NOT mean:

**"No."**

### Checked

Means:

**"Customer confirmed this."**

### I don't know

Means:

**"Customer is unsure."**

The AI must never assume that an unchecked item was inspected.

For example:

If the checklist says:

☐ Does the dashboard turn on?

and the customer leaves it unchecked, the AI must NOT say:

**"Dashboard does not turn on."**

Instead it must understand:

**"Dashboard status is unknown."**
